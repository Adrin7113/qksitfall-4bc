# A miniature charging lot

Figure tier. A solid two-bay charging-lot model moves the cars between waiting,
charging, and departure positions as the reader inspects a reservation slot.
The reservation board and five-bit energy inspector use the same booking model.

The object is literal: one port in each bay, one car at a time. The miniature
does not pretend to be a quantum computer. A separate, clearly labeled proposed
QAOA walkthrough explains how its bookings become a cost Hamiltonian.

## Purposeful parts, before geometry

- Base: four supporting feet, beveled plinth, asphalt inset, boundary groove,
  four corner fasteners, two curb rails, entrance apron (14 parts).
- Each bay: parking outline, wheel stop, charger foundation, charger body,
  charger cap, recessed screen, screen face, indicator, button plate, two
  fixing points (11 parts per bay).
- Each car: four tire blocks, undercarriage, body, bonnet, cabin, roof,
  windshield, rear glass, two mirrors, two lamps, rear bumper (16 parts each).
- Context: four boundary bollards and a small engraved equipment plate.

The rounded tires are toy-model blocks rather than detailed curved mechanical
parts. All geometry is Anatomy slab primitives with closed outlines; there are
no pipes, cables, lathes, or moving joints. The pipe/lathe geometry audit is
therefore inapplicable. Screen-line and responsive visual checks still apply.

At 1× the model reads as a charging lot. At 4× the plinth bevel, charger screen
recesses, mirror mounts, windows, fasteners, and vehicle body seams explain how
the model is assembled. The two stations are identified in the external legend
and reservation board, keeping words out of the drawing.
